# Team and Roles

- **Team name:** KIDS26 Team 4
- **Project focus:** Support the Hartwell Center's production NGS run planner with historical sequencing-quality data in PostgreSQL.
- **Expected output:** Reproducible imports for NovaSeq X Plus, BCL-Convert, and TapeStation exports that support R Shiny loading recommendations.
- **Tools and stack:** Python, pandas, psycopg, PostgreSQL, and R Shiny.
